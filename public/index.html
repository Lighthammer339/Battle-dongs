HOW TO PLAY

  • THE DRAFT: before the fight everyone takes turns picking powers from a shared pool. Pick smart — what you don't take, they get.
  • YOUR TURN: swing the CROSSHAIR, pick a power, then HOLD SPACE to work up a load — let go to shoot. The longer you hold, the further it flies. Max it out and it goes off by itself. No wind, no excuses.
  • HEALTH: every dong has 150. Run out and you die — last dong standing wins. Your wood shows it: rock hard when healthy, limp when you're about to go.
  • CRATES: supplies parachute in during the fight — HEALTH, a mystery POWER, or VIAGRA (next shot hits 1.5x). Walk over one or shoot it to grab it.
  • SCORE: every point of damage you deal is a point for you. Hit yourself and you lose those points.
  • THE GROUND gets blown apart and collapses. Dig them into pits, bury them in dirt, or drop the ground out from under them.
  • MOVES: you get 4 moves per game to drive your tank left or right. Use them wisely.
  • If nobody's dead by the time everyone's out of powers, the highest score among the living wins. Kills are worth +25.

CONTROLS

UP / DOWN = swing the crosshair (hold SHIFT for fine aim) · LEFT / RIGHT = face that way
HOLD SPACE = charge, release = FIRE · ENTER = fire at your last power · A / D = drive
W / S = switch power · click / drag on the field to point the crosshair · ESC = pause · M = music

Every power and what it does lives in powers.txt next to the server — edit it to make your own.
